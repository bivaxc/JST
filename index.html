About the terminal

Stewardship of a finite resource.

JST is a bonded petroleum storage facility operated by Uganda National Oil Company (UNOC). It provides storage services for oil marketing companies and holds government strategic stock to support security of petroleum supply.

Petroleum products are non-renewable: reserves take geological time to form and cannot be replenished on a human timescale. The terminal’s role is therefore one of responsible storage, stock accountability, quality preservation and efficient movement—not endless supply.

Established in the 1970s as a strategic reserve, JST has been managed by UNOC since 2017. UNOC took over full management and operatorship in December 2022.

Innovation at JST · concept

ADS: Automated Dipping System.

ADS is a proposed digital way to make tank stock checks more consistent. Automated readings, recorded with timestamps, can help operations teams compare measured stock with receipts and dispatches, spot unusual variances sooner, and keep a clearer audit trail. It supports careful stewardship of every litre and safer routine checks.

Designed for the next step: integrate tank readings with inventory records and reporting, while keeping trained operators responsible for verification and release decisions. This is a concept description; it does not claim that an ADS is currently installed or operating at JST.

OMC customer portal

Register your business. Place an order.

Fuel ordering is available to registered Oil Marketing Companies. Create a company account with its Uganda Revenue Authority TIN, then submit a product and quantity request from the same account area.

Products: PMS (petrol) and AGO (diesel). Order requests are subject to business verification, available stock, product terms and UNOC approval.

This portal stores account details and order requests in a local database on the computer running the server. It is not connected to UNOC or URA; company registration and TINs require manual verification, and each order request needs UNOC confirmation.
